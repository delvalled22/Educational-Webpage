Tennis Strategies

Offensive Tennis Strategies

  • Serve aggressively to take control of the point early and force your opponent into a defensive position.
  • Use powerful groundstrokes to push your opponent deep behind the baseline and create openings.
  • Approach the net after a strong shot to finish the point with volleys or overheads.
  • Attack short balls with a strong inside-out forehand to keep the opponent off balance.
  • Mix pace and direction to prevent your opponent from settling into a rhythm and to create attacking opportunities.

Defensive Tennis Strategies

  • Track your opponent's patterns and return deep shots consistently to keep them pinned behind the baseline.
  • Use slice and drop shots to disrupt pace and buy time to recover to a strong court position.
  • When put into a defensive position, on the run, lob to create space and reset the rally.
  • Focus on footwork and court coverage so you can reach more balls and extend rallies effectively.
  • Stay patient and let the opponent make mistakes by returning every ball and avoiding unnecessary risk.
