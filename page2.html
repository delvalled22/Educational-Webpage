Tennis Match Strategy

Offensive Patterns

  • Serve wide or into the body to open the court and make the next shot easier to attack.
  • Use deep crosscourt groundstrokes to move the opponent, then redirect the ball down the line into open space.
  • Attack short balls early so the opponent has less time to recover and reorganize their court position.
  • Follow a strong approach shot to the net when the opponent is stretched or forced to hit upward.
  • Change pace at the right moment so a fast rally becomes an opportunity instead of a predictable exchange.

Defensive Adjustments

  • Recover toward a balanced court position after every shot so the next ball is easier to track and return.
  • Hit higher, deeper neutral balls when pulled out of position to buy time and reset the rally.
  • Use a slice backhand to slow the tempo and force the opponent to generate their own pace.
  • Lob over an advancing opponent when passing lanes are small and the net player is crowding space.
  • Stay patient in long rallies because many points are won by disciplined decision-making rather than one spectacular shot.
