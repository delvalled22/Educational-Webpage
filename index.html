Types of Tennis Shots

To be a complete tennis player, you need to master various types of shots.

  1. Forehand - hit on the same side as your dominant hand
  2. Backhand - hit on the opposite side of your dominant hand
  3. Serve - the initial shot that starts each point
  4. Volley - hit the ball before it bounces
  5. Drop Shot - a soft shot that lands close to the net
  6. Lob - a high shot hit over the opponent's head
  7. Smash - a powerful overhead shot hit when the ball is high in the air

In addition to your tennis shots, there are numerous tennis strategies to master.

Tennis strategies can significantly impact your success on the court, and helps you adapt to different playing styles and situations.
